# RRaw Viewer

A fast RAW photo viewer and culler for Mac. Opens RAW files from almost every camera instantly, lets you sort with colour tags (which Lightroom and Capture One read as star ratings) and colour your folders, import from SD cards and cameras, compare two photos side by side, and move, copy and rename photos safely. Your photos are never overwritten and nothing is deleted without going to the Trash first.

## New in 1.49

- **Colour your folders.** Click a folder once and press **1–7** (red, orange, yellow, green, blue, purple, gray). The folder turns that colour, and Finder sees the same colour tag. Another number changes the colour, the same number takes it off, and **⌘Z** undoes.
- **⌘F** goes straight to the search box again, in every view.

## Install (one time)

1. Double-click **RRawViewer.dmg**.
2. Drag **RRaw Viewer** onto the **Applications** folder.
3. Open it from Applications. That's it: RRaw Viewer is signed and approved by Apple, so it opens with no security warning.

## Updates

You don't need to come back here. When there's a new version, a card pops up in the middle of RRaw Viewer. Click **Update Now** and it updates itself in a few seconds and reopens. You can also choose **RRaw Viewer ▸ Check for Updates…** any time.

## Privacy

RRaw Viewer collects nothing: no accounts, no analytics, no tracking. It only goes online to look for updates on this page, and that check sends nothing about you, your Mac or your photos. GitHub, which hosts this page and the downloads, sees those requests like any web visit ([GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)).
