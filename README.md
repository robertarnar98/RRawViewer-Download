# RRaw Viewer

A fast RAW photo viewer and culler for Mac. Opens RAW files from almost every camera instantly, lets you sort with colour tags (which Lightroom and Capture One read as star ratings), import from SD cards and cameras, compare two photos side by side, and move, copy and rename photos safely. Your photos are never overwritten and nothing is deleted without going to the Trash first.

## Download

**[⬇︎ Download the newest version](https://github.com/robertarnar98/RRawViewer-Download/releases/latest)**: click **RRawViewer.dmg** under *Assets*.

## Install (one time)

1. Double-click **RRawViewer.dmg**.
2. Drag **RRaw Viewer** onto the **Applications** folder.
3. Open it from Applications. The first time, macOS says it can't verify the app: click **Done**, then go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway**.

## Updates

You don't need to come back here. When there's a new version, a card pops up in the middle of RRaw Viewer. Click **Update Now** and it updates itself in a few seconds and reopens. You can also choose **RRaw Viewer ▸ Check for Updates…** any time.
